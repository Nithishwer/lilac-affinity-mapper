How to read the data · provenance and limitations

What defines a series

Each series uses a structural seed, a UniProt accession and a normalized publication DOI. Analogues share the seed’s nonempty Murcko scaffold. The mapper requires at least three distinct measured structures including the seed. Ring-changing and acyclic analogues are outside this screen. Series can overlap across seeds and studies.

Assay evidence

“Same assay” is the mapper’s ChEMBL assay-ID comparison for the same endpoint; “unknown” includes BindingDB and unidentified seed assays. It is not evidence of independent replication. The seed affinity table has no assay-ID column. Binding and functional ChEMBL assays are included upstream. The evidence snapshot now adds assay descriptions, B/F labels, organism, cell, tissue and subcellular-fraction fields from ChEMBL. An assay-organism field does not by itself identify the expression host or biological preparation.

Values and duplicate records

IC50, Ki and Kd are in nM; pIC50, pKi and pKd remain logarithmic. Endpoints are displayed separately. Qualifiers are preserved, with the inequality reversed on conversion from concentration to p-value. Bounds and approximate values are excluded from exact ranges. All database records are retained. Compound counts use distinct SMILES; identical marks overlap without averaging or deduplicating the source tables.

Membrane annotations

The local LILAC structures are OPM/PPM-oriented, with the bilayer midplane at Z = 0. The shaded region is the reported lipid-tail region, ± the half thickness. Exposure uses the supplied per-atom touches_bilayer field; it is not recomputed from Z. All percentages use non-hydrogen atoms. Seed exposure is not transferred to analogues. PDB protein names and source species describe the structure, including fusion constructs, and may differ from assay targets.
